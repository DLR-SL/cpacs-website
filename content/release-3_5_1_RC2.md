Title: CPACS v3.5.1-RC2 available for review
Date: 2026-07-04 10:00
Category: Releases
Author: Marko

CPACS v3.5.1-RC2 is now available for community review.

This second release candidate provides an updated review state for CPACS v3.5.1 and focuses in particular on the further refinement of the predefined `systemElements`. Compared to the first release candidate, the `systemElements` definition has been significantly elaborated and is now organized in a clearer function-oriented hierarchy.

The updated structure groups predefined system elements according to their primary functional role, for example electrical, mechanical, pneumatic, hydraulic, and thermo-fluid elements. Within these domains, elements are further organized into functional categories such as storage, conversion, distribution, control, and consumers where applicable.

Relevant links:

- [CPACS releases](https://github.com/DLR-SL/CPACS/releases)
- [CPACS v3.5.1-RC2 documentation](https://dlr-sl.github.io/CPACS/html/88830dfb-9468-15e9-2772-2eb249b0a0ac.htm)
- [Discussion and feedback thread](https://github.com/DLR-SL/CPACS/discussions/862)

We kindly invite the CPACS community to review the updated `systemElements` definition and provide feedback by **28 July 2026**.

In particular, we would appreciate feedback on whether the new functional grouping is clear and intuitive, whether the naming is understandable and consistent, and whether the updated structure raises any migration or implementation concerns for existing tools and data sets.

Unless major concerns are raised during this review period, the feedback received by 28 July will be used to finalize CPACS v3.5.1.

Many thanks to everyone who contributed to the current schema refinements and extensions!
